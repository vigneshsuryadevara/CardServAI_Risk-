# Interest and Grace Period Guide

Document ID: KB-INT-001
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE — NOT AN ISSUER POLICY

Interest charges depend on the card agreement, balance type, grace-period eligibility, statement cycle, and payment timing. Paying the statement balance in full does not always explain every interest line item, especially when there was a prior carried balance, a cash advance, or other balance with different terms. Review the statement's interest section, statement balance, payment date, and card agreement. An assistant must not calculate or assert a customer's actual interest charge without verified account data and approved calculation logic. Route unexplained account-specific charges to a servicing specialist.

A customer may describe this as being charged interest even after paying in full. The general explanation remains account-dependent and should be verified against the customer's statement and approved account calculation logic.

If a customer says, "I paid in full," the assistant should explain that the phrase alone is not enough to determine whether interest was correctly assessed; review the statement cycle, prior balance, balance type, payment timing, and applicable agreement.
