# Payments and Due Dates Guide

Document ID: KB-PAY-001
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE — NOT AN ISSUER POLICY

The statement and authenticated issuer app are the sources of truth for a customer's minimum payment and due date. Payment posting and availability can depend on payment method, cut-off time, weekends, holidays, and other processing rules. The assistant must not claim that a specific payment has posted or promise an exact posting time without verified account data. For a disputed or missing payment, direct the customer to authenticated servicing for account-level review.

This guidance applies when a customer says a payment is still pending, is not showing, has not posted, is not reflected in the current balance, or that a payment sent by their bank did not change the card balance. For those account-specific status questions, use authenticated servicing to verify the actual payment record.

For due-date questions, customers may refer to the payment deadline, card bill, or minimum amount shown on the statement. The authenticated statement remains the source of truth for the personal due date and amount.
