# Card Security Guide

Document ID: KB-SEC-001
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE — NOT AN ISSUER POLICY

If a card is lost, stolen, or potentially compromised, advise the customer to use the issuer's official app or verified contact channel immediately to lock the card or report the issue. The demo assistant cannot lock, cancel, or replace a card. Never request a full card number, password, PIN, or one-time passcode. Suspected fraud or compromised credentials should be routed to the official secure process.

This guidance also applies when the customer says their card is missing, cannot find the card, received a suspicious charge, or may have experienced account compromise.

Customers may describe risk as someone copying their payment details or several unfamiliar purchases they cannot account for. These should be directed to the official secure fraud-reporting process.
