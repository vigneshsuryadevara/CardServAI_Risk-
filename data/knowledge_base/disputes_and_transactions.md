# Transactions and Disputes Guide

Document ID: KB-DIS-001
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE — NOT AN ISSUER POLICY

For a transaction the customer does not recognize or believes is incorrect, direct them to the issuer's official authenticated dispute or fraud-reporting channel. Encourage them to keep relevant receipts and transaction details. Do not invent dispute deadlines, promise an outcome, or ask the customer to provide full card numbers, passwords, PINs, or one-time passcodes in chat. A transaction-specific issue requires secure account-level review.

The workflow also applies when a merchant appears to have charged the customer more than once, when a transaction amount looks incorrect, or when the customer wants to report a purchase they did not make. If the customer describes likely fraud or compromised card details, use the official fraud-reporting route.

If the customer asks about an unauthorized charge, or requests removal of a charge they do not recognize, treat it as a transaction-specific issue and direct them to the authenticated dispute or fraud-reporting process. Do not state that a charge has been removed.

Examples include a merchant taking the same amount twice, a duplicate merchant authorization, an incorrect merchant amount, an unidentifiable purchase, or a purchase that was not made by the customer.
