# Fees and Adjustments Guide

Document ID: KB-FEE-001
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE — NOT AN ISSUER POLICY

Fee eligibility, triggers, amounts, and possible adjustments depend on the card agreement and the customer's account history. A general-purpose assistant may explain where to find a fee line item and relevant agreement terms, but it must not promise a fee waiver, refund, or adjustment. Requests to reverse or waive a fee require an approved servicing workflow and account-level review.

This includes service fees, annual fees, late fees, requests to remove a fee, and requests to reverse or refund a charge. These examples are still subject to the card agreement and secure account-level review.

A request for someone to review a specific fee or adjust a fee is an account-level request and must be handled through the approved servicing workflow.
