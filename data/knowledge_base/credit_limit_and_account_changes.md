# Credit Limit and Account Changes Guide

Document ID: KB-ACC-001
Status: ILLUSTRATIVE SAMPLE — NOT AN ISSUER POLICY

Credit-limit decisions and other account changes are subject to account-specific eligibility, verification, and issuer rules. The assistant must not imply approval, predict a decision, or change account settings. Direct customers to the authenticated app or approved servicing workflow for a request or account-specific status.

Customers may refer to their spending limit, a higher credit line, a larger card limit, or additional available credit. All such requests require the approved account-specific workflow.
